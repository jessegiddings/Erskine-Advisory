## The engagement

Full representation places one party on the project whose only obligation is to you, from design development through the end of warranty. We do not perform construction work, we do not hire trades, and we hold no contract with the builder — you contract with your contractor directly. Our role is to advise, review, verify, coordinate, and report, continuously, on your side of that line.

## What it includes

- **Everything in the [Pre-Construction Audit](/services/pre-construction-audit)**, if the engagement begins before signatures — the audit fee is credited against the full engagement.
- **Draw verification.** Every payment application checked against work in place before release, with site verification by retained independent inspectors in the project's region.
- **Change-order discipline.** Each change reviewed and independently priced where warranted, and — more importantly — a running ledger of the aggregate against budget and contingency, so the total is a number you watch rather than a surprise you inherit.
- **Schedule oversight.** Progress tracked against a baseline with logic, so slippage is identified when it is a conversation, not a claim.
- **Consultant coordination.** The architect, engineers, and specialist consultants coordinated around your interest, with decisions documented and open items driven to closure.
- **The owner's report.** A standing plain-language report on a fixed cadence: where the money is, where the schedule is, what changed, what needs your decision. You should never learn something important about your own project by accident.
- **Closeout and warranty.** Deficiencies driven to completion, holdbacks and lien periods managed, warranties documented and pursued while the obligations are live.

## How it is priced

Quoted per engagement, after scope is understood — as a percentage of construction cost that steps down as project value rises, or as a monthly retainer for long or uncertain timelines. The structure and the refusals behind it are published on [the fees page](/fees). The fee is agreed in writing before the engagement begins and is never contingent on what the project spends.

## Where it starts

Almost always with the [Pre-Construction Audit](/services/pre-construction-audit) or a [project review](/project-review). Nobody should hand a stranger a multi-year engagement, and we do not ask anyone to.
