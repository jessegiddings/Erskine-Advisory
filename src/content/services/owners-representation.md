## The problem it solves

A significant residential project runs two to three years, involves dozens of firms, and generates thousands of transactions and decisions. Every professional on it is paid by someone — and none of them holds, as their actual job, the owner's whole position: cost, schedule, quality and risk, together, on the owner's side only.

Full representation puts one party at the table with exactly that job, from design development through warranty and final reconciliation. We perform no construction work and hold no contract with the builder — you sign with your general contractor directly. Our role is to advise, review, verify, coordinate and report, continuously, on your side of that line.

## What's included

- Everything in the [Pre-Construction Audit](/services/pre-construction-audit), when the engagement begins before signatures — with the audit fee credited.
- **Bid comparison and contract negotiation support** with your counsel: redlines, bid analysis, a scope responsibility matrix.
- **Meetings chaired and minuted, the decision log owned** — so decisions are made once, in writing, and stay made.
- **Every payment application read against work in place** before approval, with site verification by retained licensed inspectors in the project's region. Draws approved in writing, or not.
- **Every change order interrogated**: in scope or new, price supported or not, schedule effect stated — and a running ledger of the aggregate against contingency.
- **A live schedule held against the real baseline**, so slippage is a recorded fact with a date rather than a dawning realization.
- **The monthly owner's report**, in plain language: where the money is, where the schedule is, what changed, what needs your decision.
- **Closeout and warranty**: punch list to closure, lien releases, as-builts, warranties documented and pursued, final reconciliation.

## Who it's for

Owners committing to a multi-year project who want representation for its full arc — especially where the owner is remote, the site is complex, or the project's governance will be reviewed by a family office.

## Fee

A percentage of construction cost billed monthly with final reconciliation, stepping down as project size rises — or a monthly retainer for open-ended scopes. Quoted per engagement after we understand the project; the structure and our refusals are published on [the fees page](/fees).

## What it isn't

Not a second builder, and not a replacement for your architect or contractor — the project still needs both doing their jobs well. Not a guarantee that nothing goes wrong; it is the structure that determines how early you find out and how strong your position is when something must be resolved.
